02 · AI For Business

Mall Customer Segmentation

Applied K-Means and Hierarchical (Agglomerative) clustering to segment mall customers by Annual Income, Age, and Spending Score. Used the elbow method and dendrograms to determine optimal cluster counts, and Pandas Profiling for automated EDA — surfacing five distinct customer personas to inform data-driven marketing strategy.

Python Scikit-Learn K-Means Hierarchical Clustering EDA Pandas Matplotlib Seaborn Plotly
03 · Regression Analysis

Automobile Price Prediction

Built and compared three OLS Multiple Linear Regression models to predict car prices for an automobile consulting company. Progressed from a single predictor (horsepower) to a multi-feature model (horsepower, curb weight, engine size), validating each with residual analysis and train/test evaluation to identify the best-fit specification.

Python statsmodels OLS Regression Scikit-Learn EDA Residual Analysis Pandas Seaborn
04 · Capstone · Time Series

Energy Consumption Forecasting for Smart Grid Sustainability

Capstone practicum project forecasting regional electricity demand to support smart grid planning. Integrated federal holiday flags and weather data, then trained and compared three models — SARIMA, XGBoost, and Facebook Prophet with holiday regressors — across multiple US regions. Applied LIME for model explainability and MinMax-scaled LSTM as an additional deep-learning baseline, enabling a rigorous multi-model evaluation of energy demand patterns by season, region, and holiday effect.

Python SARIMA XGBoost Facebook Prophet LSTM LIME TensorFlow / Keras Time Series Forecasting Scikit-Learn Energy Analytics Smart Grid Pandas Matplotlib
